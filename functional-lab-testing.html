The short answer

Allure offers functional lab testing and InBody body-composition scans in Plantation, FL. Five panels cover hormones, metabolism and thyroid, vitamins and minerals, peptides, and gut health. Results are reviewed with you and turned into a nutrition, supplement or medical plan. Testing is self-pay.

Frequently asked

Your provider will go over anything specific to you at your consultation.

How is this different from my yearly physical?

Standard panels look for disease. Broader panels add markers that help us fine-tune nutrition, hormones and energy.

Will this diagnose a condition?

No. Results guide your wellness plan. If something needs medical follow-up, we'll tell you and refer you.

Do I need to fast?

Some panels require fasting. We'll tell you when you book.

Is insurance accepted?

Testing is self-pay. Ask us about pricing when you book.
