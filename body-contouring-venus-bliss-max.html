The short answer

Venus Bliss MAX at Allure in Plantation, FL combines diode laser fat reduction, radiofrequency skin tightening and muscle stimulation. Sessions take 30 to 45 minutes with no downtime. Most plans are 4 to 8 weekly sessions, and results develop over 8 to 12 weeks.

How it works

  1. Consultation

    We look at the area, your goals and your history to plan your sessions.

  2. Weekly sessions

    Most plans run 4 to 8 sessions about a week apart.

  3. Track progress

    We take photos at the start and along the way so you can see the change.

Frequently asked

Your provider will go over anything specific to you at your consultation.

When will I see a difference?

Some clients notice early changes in 2 to 3 weeks. Results keep developing over 8 to 12 weeks. Everyone responds differently.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as warm and comfortable. No numbing is needed.

How long do results last?

They last best when your weight stays stable. Maintenance sessions can help.

Is it a weight-loss treatment?

No. It's for shaping specific areas. For weight loss, see our medical weight-loss program.
