Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call 954-228-5034 and we'll help you choose.

Do I need a consultation first?

For medical weight loss, body contouring and lab testing, yes. It's how we make sure a treatment is right for you. Facials and most IV drips can be booked directly.

Who performs the treatments?

Medical treatments are evaluated and overseen by Alicia Jimenez, a board-certified APRN. Facials and Venus Bliss MAX are performed by our licensed esthetician.

Where are you located?

8320 W Sunrise Blvd, Suite 211, in Plantation, near Fort Lauderdale, Sunrise and Davie. Parking is free on site.

Do you offer memberships or packages?

Yes. Our Allure membership gives you a monthly treatment and member pricing, and most treatments have package pricing.

Can I pay over time?

Monthly payment plans are available for packages and weight-loss programs. Ask us when you book.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. You'll see the full policy when you book.
