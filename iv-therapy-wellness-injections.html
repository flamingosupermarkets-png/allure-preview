Frequently asked

Your provider will go over anything specific to you at your consultation.

Is IV therapy safe?

It's generally well tolerated by healthy adults when given after a health screening. Bruising or vein irritation can happen. People with some heart or kidney conditions, or who are pregnant, should talk with a provider first.

How often can I get a drip?

It depends on your goals and health. Some clients come once before an event; others come on a regular schedule your provider recommends.

Does it help a hangover?

It can rehydrate you and may help you feel better. It isn't a cure.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Booking ahead makes sure a provider and a chair are ready for you.
