The short answer

Allure offers one-on-one nutrition coaching in Plantation, FL with Vanessa Gonzalez, a National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach. Plans cover eating, sleep and supplements, can be built from your lab results, and work on their own or alongside medical weight loss. No referral is needed.

How it works

  1. First session

    We go over your health history, eating patterns, schedule and goals.

  2. Your plan

    You get a written nutrition plan and, if useful, a supplement protocol.

  3. Check-ins

    Regular sessions to adjust the plan as your life and results change.

Frequently asked

Your provider will go over anything specific to you at your consultation.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly.

Can I do this with medical weight loss?

Yes. Many clients pair the two so the habits last after medication.

Are supplements required?

No. We recommend them only when they fit your goals and labs.

How often do we meet?

Most clients check in monthly, but we'll set a schedule that suits you.
